# History with AI

This website will take you through a syllabus for a college course titled *History with AI*. The course is designed to be a semester long, with 75-minute meetings two days a week. 

Use the navigation bar at the top of the website to see different views of the syllabus, an AI Usage Statement and a bibliography.
